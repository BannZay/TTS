Voice: Kokoro-82M (af_heart), Apache License 2.0, https://huggingface.co/hexgrad/Kokoro-82M
The clips are audio generated with this model, then cut into units, level-adjusted and re-encoded to Ogg Vorbis.
A copy of the Apache License 2.0 is in LICENSE-Apache-2.0.txt.
The model card names CC BY audio in the Kokoro v1.0 training data: Koniwa tnc (CC BY 3.0) and SIWIS (CC BY 4.0).
Phonemes: CMU Pronouncing Dictionary. Clips were rendered from dictionary pronunciations, cut into units by tools/build_kokoro.py and sped up to the tempo of mid-phrase clips by tools/build_kokoro_endings.py.
